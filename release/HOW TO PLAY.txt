DwarfClone
==========

A fortress-building game in the terminal: dig into a mountain, keep a
band of dwarves fed, housed and happy, and survive whatever the world
throws at them. Losing is fun.

STARTING THE GAME
-----------------
1. Put this folder somewhere you can write to (Documents or the Desktop,
   not Program Files). Saves go in a "saves" folder next to the game.
2. Double-click dwarfclone.exe. The game opens in its own window of
   square tiles, where walls, water and floors join into a solid map.
   Ctrl and + or - (or Ctrl and the mouse wheel) zoom the tiles. Text
   uses Cascadia Mono or Consolas; --font PATH picks another .ttf.
3. To play in a terminal instead: open Windows Terminal, make it at
   least 130 x 40 characters, and run  .\dwarfclone.exe --terminal

FIRST STEPS
-----------
- New world: pick settings (or just press Enter), watch history play out,
  accept, then choose a site on the world map and press Enter. A card shows
  the land and its neighbours: Play now takes the usual party and wagon,
  Prepare carefully lets you spend points on skills and supplies first.
- Mouse: drag on the map to mark a box (a click marks one tile);
  Ctrl+Z undoes the last designation.
- Press ? at any time for the key list.
- Space pauses and unpauses. + and - change speed.
- d digs, b builds, i makes zones (farms, meeting hall, bedrooms...),
  p makes stockpiles, k looks at what's under the cursor, z is the status
  screen, n is nobles and posts, m is the military, c is the chronicle.

SOUND
-----
- Music, ambience and effects are made by the game as it plays. M mutes
  or unmutes at any time; O (or Sound on the title menu) sets the
  volumes. No speakers? The game simply runs silently.

DON'T WANT TO PLAY? WATCH
-------------------------
- "Watch a fort" on the title screen: an AI overseer builds and runs a
  fortress for you, in one of five styles (Steady, Builder, Delver,
  Warlord, Reckless). Most end in ruin.
- Press A in any fortress to hand it to the overseer (or take it back).
- From a terminal, to have it play ten fortresses and report how each
  ended:
      .\dwarfclone.exe --autoplay --runs 10 --years 5
